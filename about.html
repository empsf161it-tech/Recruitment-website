Our Story

Built on a
Belief in Better Hiring

TalentForge was founded by Alexandra Reid and James Okafor — two former investment bankers who had experienced firsthand how broken the hiring process was. Companies waited months for candidates who left within a year. Talented people accepted the wrong roles because they lacked access to the right opportunities.

Their answer was to create something different: a recruitment firm grounded in behavioral science, powered by proprietary technology, and driven by long-term relationships over transactional wins. Eighteen years later, that belief remains the foundation of everything we do.

Our Journey

18 Years of Milestones

Key moments that shaped TalentForge into the global leader it is today.

TalentForge Founded

Alexandra Reid and James Okafor launch TalentForge from a co-working space in Midtown Manhattan with three client briefs and a bold vision.

First 1,000 Placements

Despite the financial crisis, TalentForge reached 1,000 successful placements and expanded to a 25-person team. Clients valued our integrity during the downturn.

London & Dubai Expansion

Opened European and Middle Eastern offices. Began serving FTSE 100 and sovereign wealth fund clients. Team grew to 80 consultants.

TalentIQ™ Platform Launch

Launched proprietary AI-powered matching platform, reducing average time-to-shortlist from 45 to 18 days. Transformative for client outcomes.

10,000 Placements Milestone

Celebrated our 10,000th placement. Launched dedicated DEI recruiting practice. Featured in Forbes' "Best Executive Search Firms" list for 4th year running.

15,000 Placements & Beyond

Surpassed 15,000 lifetime placements. Expanded to APAC. Launched TalentForge Academy for employer hiring capability building. The journey continues.
